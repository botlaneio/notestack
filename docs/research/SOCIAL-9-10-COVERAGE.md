## Source corrections and verification

- Unpaid care and voluntary services can be purposeful productive work. Do not generalise the source's recreation/purposeless wording to all unpaid activity. Separate consent from coerced labour. Training labels do not imply human worth.
- Statistical unemployment is not every absence of employment. Separate seeking, availability, reference period and underemployment; [ILO definitions](https://www.ilo.org/resource/labour-underutilization). Do not infer criminality or unwillingness from joblessness. Rural/urban skill-shortage statements are claims to investigate, not universal current facts.
- The historical statute is **Equal Remuneration Act, 1976**. Later Code on Wages context is necessary: [21 November 2025 commencement notification](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/267885.pdf), [Ministry/PIB account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2192463). Organised does not guarantee every benefit; informal does not mean law-free. Applicable arrangements, thresholds and rules need verification in real cases.
- India is in **South Asia**, despite the first paragraph's South-East Asia. New Delhi is a city within the NCT of Delhi. Population/share figures are dated; distinguish area, distance, count and rank.
- **82°30′E = 82.5°E**, near Mirzapur; it does not pass through Prayagraj. All India uses IST (UTC+5:30); the idealised 4 minutes/degree model is not a set of new official time zones.
- Preserve textbook mainland coastline answer **6,100 km**, source total **7,516.6 km**. The [official 29 April 2025 reassessment](https://shipmin.gov.in/sites/default/files/Length%20of%20Indias%20Coastline%20Circular_0.pdf) gives total **11,098.81 km** under a changed measurement method. It does not mean that much coast was physically added. [MHA listing](https://www.mha.gov.in/index.php/border-management-ii-division) gives land border **15,106.7 km**, distinct from the book's internally summed **15,200 km** table.
- Named lines need scope: Radcliffe/partition, McMahon/eastern India–China sector and dispute, Durand/Afghanistan–present Pakistan. Distinguish territorial claims and administration for K2 and the Afghanistan-frontier account. The app does not adjudicate boundaries; link official maps and teacher guidance.
- Source Everest **8,848 m** is an older figure than **8,848.86 m** ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2131086)). Source Himadri height bands are broad descriptions, not maximum peak heights. Gangotri feeds Bhagirathi, with Ganga named below Devprayag. Chaukhamba is not a standard dun example. The plains' 2,400 km refers to length. The “recently formed landmass” exercise is ambiguous about system/range/deposit scale; teacher confirmation is retained rather than automatically certifying one short answer.
- Bhabar is the standard form of source “Babar”. Pulicat spans Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu. Coast-region labels are simplified. Andaman–Nicobar is an island-arc setting with volcanic features, not uniform lava rock. The book's 247 = 204 + 43 is not a verified current total; [Survey of India inventory/criteria](https://www.surveyofindia.gov.in/UserFiles/files/list%20of%20Offshore%20and%20inshore%20Islands.pdf).

## Map provenance

`src/lib/india-outline-learning.json` derives from [Survey of India outline vector](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/outline-maps-of-india), downloaded from `https://surveyofindia.gov.in/documents/Outline_of_India.zip`. File metadata is February 2026. Original LCC WGS84 geometry transformed to longitude/latitude, topology-preserving simplification at 0.025°, then projected into a learning SVG. 1,151 polygons remain. This is generalised learning geometry, not navigation or a precise regional-boundary overlay. Dots are approximate representative locations and do not depict complete range/region extents.

The [official political map](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/political-map-of-india/1000), [outline](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/outline-maps-of-india) and [physical map](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/physical-map-of-india) are linked for actual drawing and height-legend work. Explanations await teacher review.
