## Critical source distinctions

- Cornwallis’s generalisation about Indians is a prejudiced colonial viewpoint,not an endorsed factual description. India had educational,judicial and political institutions before the Company; centralisation did not itself create all national unity.
- Fort William College1800versus source1801;1853competitive-recruitment provision versus examinations1855. Senior access remained unequal. Police titles and military-reform dates are qualified; the post-revolt Peel process is1858–1859rather than completed1857recommendations. Court plan1772is distinct from Diwani1765.
- Permanent Company demand is distinct from cultivator rent. Mahal means a revenue unit,often village/estate,not automatically modern taluk. Ryotwari source50%and30-year figures are explicitly simplified,not universal regional rules. Direct settlement did not remove risk of debt or land loss.
- Banaras Sanskrit College is generally dated1791rather than source1792. Macaulay’s1835Minute describes a colonial aim,not a measure of Indian intelligence. Wood’s Despatch1854differs from the three1857universities under Canning. Schooling was not instantly universal.
-1773Governor-General of Bengal differs from1833Governor-General of India. Pitt’s Board of Control did not abolish the Court of Directors.1813retained tea/China monopoly exceptions;1833ended remaining Company commerce.1858Crown transfer differs from the1Novemberproclamation; Governor-General office continued alongside the Viceroy role.
- Nomination1861,budget discussion1892,and limited/separate electorates1909are not universal democracy.1919provincial dyarchy is not central bicameralism. Montagu’s1917policy announcement is not attributed solely to Chelmsford.
-1935federation/central dyarchy were proposed but never operated. Provincial autonomy and Federal Court operated from1937with restrictions. RBI opened1935under RBI Act1934,not the Government of India Act1935. Later constitutional influence is selective transformation,not a claim the independent Constitution is an unchanged colonial Act.
- Wodeyar origin story is a dynastic tradition. Commissioners1831–1881,restoration1881,minor accession1894/personal charge1902,and later Maharaja/Rajpramukh/Governor offices differ. Institutional projects span administrations; unqualified electrification-priority and sole-founder claims are not taught as settled facts.
- Mysore rockets were important developments,not the first missiles anywhere. Madras1769,Mangalore1784,Srirangapatna1792and1799settlement remain separate. Source₹3crore indemnity is attributed alongside the commonly cited₹3.3crore discrepancy. Neighbouring Maratha shading is not automatically a new1799grant; Company/Nizam/reduced-Wodeyar outcomes are explained.
- Dondiya’s chronology distinguishes Governor-General Wellesley from field commander Arthur Wellesley. Kittur1824precedes Dalhousie1848. Rayanna’s resistance phase differs from execution26January1831. Koppal has no precise date in the extract; Venkatappa’s end is explicitly uncertain.
