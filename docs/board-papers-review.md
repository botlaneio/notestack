# Board papers: answers to double-check

The questions in `src/lib/board-papers/` are transcribed from the official KSEAB papers. KSEAB published key answers only for the 2022 papers, so the 2024 and 2025 worked answers are NoteStack's own. These items involved a judgement call and are worth a teacher's look first.

| Paper | Question | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Science 2025 | Q6 | Answer depends on reading the figure: we say P is anticlockwise and Q clockwise. |
| Science 2024 | Q8 | We read the eye figure as hypermetropia, so a −2.0 D (concave) lens is not suitable. |
| Science 2024 | Q24 | The paper shows the equation as a figure; our answer uses ethene + hydrogen with a nickel catalyst. |
| Maths 2024 | Q25 OR | As printed, (x³ + 4x² + 5x − 6) ÷ (x² − x + 2) leaves a remainder, so g(x) does not come out exactly. The answer says so and shows the method. |
| Social Science 2025 | whole paper | The PDF's page headers say 27.03.2024 (a template slip on KSEAB's side). We use 29 March 2025, the date in the 2025 timetable. |
| Social Science 2024 | Q3 | Red soil (B) for the largest area of the peninsular plateau. |
| Social Science 2022 | Q6 | Nagarjuna Sagar sanctuary: only Telangana (D) fits the options, though most of the reserve is in Andhra Pradesh. Compare with the 2022 key. |
| Social Science (all) | chapter tags | A few tags are a choice between two chapters, e.g. GIS/GPS under Transport and Communication, ISRO under Major Industries, Ambedkar's movements under The Freedom Struggle. |

Topics that are not course chapters (for example Our Environment, Periodic Classification, Sources of Energy) are tagged `ch: null` with a `topic`, so they show only in the full papers.
