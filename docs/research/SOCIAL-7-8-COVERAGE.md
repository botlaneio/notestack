## Accuracy decisions

- Literacy: book answer 74.04% is provisional Census 2011, ages 7+; final 72.98% is labelled separately. The source's 2001 66% differs from official 64.83/64.84%. Older age bands and approximate independence claims are not an identical series. [Census provisional release](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/42611/download/46274/Census%20of%20India%202011-Provisional%20Population%20Totals.pdf), [official comparison](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/173/AU55.pdf?source=pqals).
- Population/source-era figures are not current estimates; working age is not employment. Demographic potential depends on health, education, skills and jobs. Poverty, inequality, price changes and criminal conduct remain distinct.
- Religious or regional identity does not itself imply hostility. Uniform civil code is presented as a source proposal/constitutional policy debate, not a proven universal remedy.
- Transgender identity, intersex traits and sexual orientation are distinct; replace the source's conflation/deficit framing. Preserve the book's 2014/2019 chronology as dated history. [2026 Amendment Gazette](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/271438.pdf) changes statutory provisions; the lesson does not decide current legal eligibility. Karnataka's 2017 policy and 2021 recruitment measure are separate.
- Women's milestones do not imply universal equality. Job rules vary. The [106th Amendment](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_parliament/2023/Constitution_106th_%28A%29_Act%2C%202023.pdf) has implementation conditions; do not infer universal immediate operation from enactment.
- Article 320(4) concerns commission consultation, not a reservation guarantee. Distinguish Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles, enabling provisions and political representation; qualify Articles 25/30. [Constitution](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/08/b9a933fb88c0a5afba254256bc55a3f0.pdf).
- Article 17 abolition differs from real-world eradication. The 1955 statute was renamed/amended in 1976 as the [Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1544/1/A1955-22Eng.pdf); the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act is dated 1989.

Source notes appear in both chapters' lessons, practice and coverage. No original PDF is published. Explanations await teacher review. Student caste, identity, family resources and personal experiences are never required.
