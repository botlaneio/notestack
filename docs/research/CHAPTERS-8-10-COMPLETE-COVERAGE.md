## Content decisions

- Chapter 8 includes all six ratio definitions, similarity justification, reciprocal and quotient relationships, the 30°/45°/60° constructions, the complete exact-value table including 0°/90°, monotonicity restricted to the chapter’s range, all three fundamental identities and every Exercise 8.3 identity proof.
- The square root in Exercise 8.3 4(vi) was checked against the rendered PDF. The printed chain in4(x) is represented with both equivalent forms and the tangent-square result.
- Undefined endpoint quantities are never represented as 0 or as an ordinary infinity value. Original denominator restrictions survive cancellation;4(iii),4(vii),4(x) explicitly exclude 45°. Positive square roots are justified by acute angles.
- Chapter 9 retains eye heights, taut/sloping lengths, sums versus differences of horizontal distances, upper structures versus total heights, and the constant-speed condition in the car problem. Exact surds are accepted; example approximations retain the book’s stated optional sqrt(3) approximation.
- Chapter 10 includes proof models for Theorems 10.1 and10.2, RHS congruence and the angle-bisector consequence, fixed-point tangent counts, normal/secant terminology, tangent lines versus finite tangent segments, concentric chords and every circumscribed-figure problem. The incircle-side model explains both area=rs and the Heron calculation.
- Interactive explorers change the reference angle and six ratios; model elevation with eye height; and move a point inside/on/outside a circle with accurate tangent contacts. Original explanatory schematics support source questions. They are marked as schematics rather than measurement drawings.

## Assessment and persistence

Written proofs, symbolic simplifications and drawing explanations are saved and explicitly self-reviewed against models. They are never automatically graded correct from their text.
